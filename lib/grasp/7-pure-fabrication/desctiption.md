# Pure Fabrication

### Problem
- Who is responsible when you are desperate, and do not want to violate high cohesion and low coupling

### Answer
- Assign a highly cohesive set of responsibilities to an artificial or convenience "behavior" class that does not represent a problem domain concept - something made up, 
in order to support high cohesion, low coupling, and reuse.

### Simplified
- Made up an entity that will be responsible for the particular logic.

### Notes
- Some entities just called real-life objects but has little in common.
- Some entities are similar to real-life objects or resemble them.
- Some objects are totally made up and are "a fabrication".

### Example
- Socket
- Connection
- Repository
- Request
- Response
- Process
- Scheduler
And many more.
